# Publication, financial claims and disclosure policy

- Paid content is explicitly marked **Paid Press Release / Third-party content**, on listings and article pages. It is never presented as independent staff reporting.
- Companies must identify an authorized representative; staff must independently verify authority before enabling public publication.
- Payment buys editorial review and the opportunity for publication subject to stated standards, not a guaranteed favorable article, index inclusion, investment interest, or regulatory compliance.
- Reviews must reject false claims, misleading guaranteed-return/investment language, impersonation, unverified financial statements, illegal promotion, mass spam, malicious links and personal data disclosures.
- Regulatory announcement requirements (e.g. accepted OTC disclosures, SEC filings) are *not fulfilled* by merely posting a page here. Integration with accepted distribution partners is out of scope until contracted.
- An approval log and corrections/retractions workflow are required operationally; staff can unpublish Wagtail Pages, but public correction notices and customer refund policies still need a formal process before selling to third parties.
- AI-generated newsroom drafts must attribute their source and remain in draft unless explicitly enabled for a particular trusted feed. Feed summaries alone are **not independent verification**. For financial and sensitive stories, use human review and cross-check original documents.
- Advertising and Google News inclusion depend on separate eligibility and review; there are no guarantees.
- Implement detailed Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, copyright/DMCA process and AML/sanctions/financial-promotion checks as appropriate before taking money from the public.
